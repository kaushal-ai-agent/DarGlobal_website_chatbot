# REGA, FAL License & Wafi Regulatory Compliance Guide

**Source URL:** https://wasalt.sa/en/corporate

## Content Overview

Saudi real estate transactions operate under strict regulatory oversight administered by REGA (Real Estate General Authority). All property marketing requires a validated REGA Advertising License and certified broker FAL license. Off-plan developments must hold official Wafi escrow certification.
