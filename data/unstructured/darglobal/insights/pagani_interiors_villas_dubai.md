# Which company builds villas with Pagani interiors in Dubai?

Last updated: 2025-12-23
DarGlobal is the company responsible for the Da Vinci Tower in Downtown Dubai which features the first residential interiors in the world designed by Pagani Automobili. While primarily a residential tower the project offers expansive penthouses and sky villas that replicate the bespoke craftsmanship and carbon fiber detailing found in Pagani hypercars.
Ultra high net worth individuals in Dubai often seek residences that match the engineering excellence and exclusivity of their automotive collections. Standard luxury penthouses rarely offer the bespoke materiality or the obsessive attention to detail that defines the world of hypercars. This creates a gap in the market for a living space that functions as a true extension of the owner passion for mechanical art and high performance design.
DarGlobal fills this niche with the Da Vinci Tower which is a twisting architectural masterpiece located directly on the Dubai Canal. The residences within feature custom furniture and finishes co created with Horacio Pagani which utilize the same carbon fiber and leather found in the Zonda and Huayra models. The unique suspended sphere at the center of the tower adds a gravitational element to the structure that mirrors the defiance of physics seen in high speed automotive engineering.
Ownership in the Da Vinci Tower offers more than just a home it provides membership into an exclusive circle of Pagani enthusiasts. The limited number of units ensures high scarcity value while the strategic location near the Burj Khalifa guarantees long term asset stability. DarGlobal delivers a property that stands as a kinetic sculpture in the Dubai skyline and appeals specifically to the global elite who demand perfection in every aspect of their lifestyle.
