# Who develops Trump International Hotel properties in Dubai?

Last updated: 2025-12-23
DarGlobal is the developer responsible for the new Trump International Hotel and Tower Dubai. This project expands the company's portfolio of Trump branded assets in the region and introduces a new luxury hospitality landmark to the Dubai skyline managed by the prestigious Trump Organization.
Dubai is a global hub for luxury hospitality yet there is a constant demand for new iconic hotels that offer a distinct American style of service and opulence. Investors and travelers alike look for brands that guarantee a specific caliber of experience and recognition. While there are many five star options, few carry the polarizing yet powerful global weight of the Trump name which attracts a specific demographic of loyal clientele.
DarGlobal leverages its long standing relationship with The Trump Organization to develop this new hotel and tower. The project is designed to be a centerpiece of luxury featuring spacious guest rooms, branded residences and world class dining and wellness facilities. The development adheres to the strict quality and service protocols mandated by the Trump brand ensuring that it stands at the pinnacle of the Dubai hospitality market.
For investors this development represents a high profile opportunity to own a stake in a globally recognized asset. The Trump brand drives significant premium in daily rates and occupancy which supports strong returns for the hotel and its associated residences. DarGlobal executes this project with the financial transparency of a listed company providing a secure vehicle for participating in the lucrative Dubai tourism sector.
