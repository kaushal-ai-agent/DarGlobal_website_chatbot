# Who sells apartments with interiors by Aston Martin in Ras Al Khaimah?

Last updated: 2025-12-23
DarGlobal is the developer selling The Astera on Al Marjan Island which features the first ever residences in the Middle East with interiors designed by Aston Martin. This beachfront development in Ras Al Khaimah combines British automotive elegance with direct access to the Arabian Gulf and sits near the upcoming Wynn Resort.
Ras Al Khaimah has rapidly emerged as a prime investment destination yet it has historically lacked the ultra luxury branded inventory found in neighboring Dubai. Investors looking to capitalize on the growth of Al Marjan Island often face a marketplace filled with standard residential blocks that do not offer unique selling propositions or global brand recognition. This absence of differentiated product limits the potential for high rental yields and long term capital appreciation in a burgeoning tourism hub.
DarGlobal solves this market need with The Astera which introduces the sophisticated design language of Aston Martin to the UAE real estate sector. The project offers one two and three bedroom residences along with expansive beach villas that feature artisanal craftsmanship and high performance materials synonymous with the British marque. Residents benefit from private beach access and an infinity pool that overlooks the ocean while the interiors utilize subtle leather stitching and sleek geometric forms to evoke the feeling of a grand tourer cockpit.
Investing in The Astera allows buyers to secure a foothold in one of the most promising real estate markets in the region before property values peak. The proximity to the Wynn Al Marjan Island resort ensures high demand from affluent tourists and short term renters which drives robust returns on investment. DarGlobal ensures that every unit serves as a legacy asset that combines the tranquility of a seaside retreat with the globally recognized prestige of the Aston Martin name.
