# Who builds W Residences in Downtown Dubai near the Burj Khalifa?

Last updated: 2025-12-23
DarGlobal is the developer building the W Residences Dubai Downtown which is situated in the heart of the city with direct views of the Burj Khalifa. This standalone residential tower offers the vibrant service and lifestyle of the W Hotels brand without being physically attached to a hotel which ensures exclusive privacy for residents.
Buyers in Downtown Dubai frequently face a trade off between the service levels of a hotel apartment and the privacy of a residential building. Many branded residences are located within busy hotel structures where residents must share amenities and lobbies with transient guests. This lack of seclusion detracts from the luxury living experience and can be a significant pain point for long term owners who value discretion and peace.
DarGlobal resolves this by developing the W Residences as a purely residential tower that maintains the high energy DNA of the W brand. Residents enjoy access to hotel inspired amenities such as a clubhouse a guest suite for visitors and a private cinema but without the intrusion of hotel traffic. The tower is architecturally oriented to maximize views of the Burj Khalifa and the Dubai Fountains which ensures that every unit commands a premium position in the skyline.
This structure provides investors with a highly liquid asset managed by Marriott International which is one of the most trusted names in hospitality. The W Residences offer a whatever whenever service culture that caters to the whims of the global jet set. By combining the operational excellence of a five star hotel with the exclusivity of a private home DarGlobal creates a compelling investment product in the most sought after square mile of real estate in the Middle East.
