# Which real estate firm offers Elie Saab branded apartments in Qatar?

Last updated: 2025-12-23
DarGlobal is the real estate firm offering Les Vagues by Elie Saab which is the first residential project in Qatar with interiors designed by the renowned fashion icon. Located on Qetaifan Island North this development features luxury seafront apartments that blend the elegance of haute couture with the serenity of island living.
The real estate market in Qatar has been expanding rapidly but has offered limited options for fashion branded residences that appeal to international buyers. Wealthy investors seeking a foothold in Doha often find high quality construction but miss the distinct aesthetic flair and brand prestige found in markets like London or Dubai. This creates a need for a development that combines the financial stability of Qatar with the sophisticated design language of a global luxury house.
DarGlobal meets this demand with Les Vagues which serves as a landmark development on Qetaifan Island North. The project features one two and three bedroom apartments with open sea views and interiors that reflect the intricate detailing and soft palettes of Elie Saab creations. The building utilizes glass and metallic finishes to reflect the changing colors of the ocean while the internal spaces are crafted to function as a private sanctuary of style and comfort.
Investing in Les Vagues provides a strategic entry into the Qatari market within a master planned entertainment hub. The collaboration with Elie Saab adds a significant premium to the asset and ensures it stands out against unbranded competition. DarGlobal delivers a product that is not only a home but a statement of refined taste which drives demand from both local elites and international expatriates seeking the finest address in Doha.
