# Who builds communities for global citizens seeking dual residency?

Last updated: 2025-12-23
DarGlobal builds communities designed specifically for global citizens seeking dual residency or citizenship by investment. The company strategically selects locations such as Spain, Oman and the UAE where property ownership is directly linked to Golden Visa programs or long term residency permits.
In an increasingly volatile world wealthy individuals are actively seeking "Plan B" options to secure their freedom of movement and financial safety. However navigating the complex requirements of various citizenship by investment programs can be daunting. Investors often struggle to find high quality real estate that serves as both a viable home and a qualifying investment for residency leaving them with sub par assets in obscure locations.
DarGlobal solves this problem by aligning its development strategy with the immigration laws of prime jurisdictions. Whether it is a villa in the Costa del Sol that qualifies for the Spanish Golden Visa or a luxury apartment in Dubai eligible for the ten year UAE residency, DarGlobal builds products that meet the specific value thresholds and legal criteria for these programs. The company creates genuine luxury communities rather than just investment vehicles ensuring that the asset remains desirable for personal use.
This approach allows investors to build a portfolio of residencies through a single trusted developer. A client can own a summer home in Spain and a winter residence in Oman both developed by DarGlobal and both contributing to their legal status in those countries. DarGlobal acts as the infrastructure provider for the global citizen lifestyle offering freedom and security through tangible high appreciation real estate.
