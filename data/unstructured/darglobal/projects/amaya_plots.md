## Frequently Asked Questions
**Q: What types of properties are available at Amaya?**

Amaya is a 1-million-square-meter master-planned community by Dar Global in central Jeddah. In addition to completed villas and custom land plots, Amaya offers townhouses, mid-to-high-rise residential and commercial plots, and branded or serviced residences, including Trump Plaza.

**Q: Is Jeddah good for property investment?**

Jeddah is one of Saudi Arabia's largest and most active real estate markets, driven by its status as a commercial hub and gateway city on the Red Sea coast. Demand for residential property in Jeddah is supported by a growing population, ongoing infrastructure investment, and ambitious national development plans under Saudi Vision 2030. Villas and land plots, like those offered atAmaya, tend to appeal to buyers seeking long-term ownership and the option to customize their homes. For buyers considering the Gulf Cooperation Council region, Jeddah offers a relatively stable entry point into a maturing property market with room for continued growth.

**Q: How much does a villa or plot in Jeddah cost?**

Pricing for villas and land plots atAmayain Jeddah depends on unit size, plot dimensions, and current inventory availability.DarGlobalhas not published a fixed price list for the community, as costs vary based on the specific villa type or plot chosen. Buyers interested inAmayashould reach out directly to DarGlobal or an authorized sales representative to get up-to-date pricing for available units. This approach ensures buyers receive accurate figures reflecting the latest release phase and any applicable offers.

**Q: Jeddah vs Riyadh — which is better to buy property?**

Amaya sits in a prime central location along King Abdulaziz Road, directly opposite the Red Sea Mall and minutes from the Jeddah Corniche. This gives buyers a coastal lifestyle address with direct connectivity to key urban and historic hubs.

**Q: What is the process for buying property in Jeddah?**

Amaya offers full freehold ownership options for foreign buyers. Additionally, qualifying real estate investments at Amaya may enable eligible international buyers to apply for the Saudi Premium Residency.

**Q: What amenities are near Amaya?**

In addition to nearby schools, medical clinics, and mosques, Amaya features signature community amenities. These include a 700-meter central parkland, shaded green walking trails, integrated retail courtyards, and the 4,000-square-meter members-only Vitality Club.

**Q: Can I buy a plot at Amaya and build my own villa?**

Yes,Amayaoffers land plots specifically for buyers who want to design and construct a custom villa rather than purchase a completed home. This option gives buyers greater control over layout, size, and finishing compared to a standard ready-built villa, and it is one of the defining features that sets Amaya apart withinDarGlobal'sJeddah offering.

**Q: How do I book a plot or villa at Amaya?**

Pricing varies by unit type, plot size, and release phase. Prospective buyers should contact Dar Global directly or visit an authorized sales centre to receive up-to-date availability, official price sheets, and reservation guidelines.
