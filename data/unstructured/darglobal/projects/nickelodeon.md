## Project Overview & Architectural Vision
DarGlobal partners with Paramount to bring Nickelodeon Hotels & Resorts to AIDA, Oman.
Set 130m above the sea, this clifftop resort blends world-class hospitality with Nickelodeon fun.

From its favorable tax environment and its strategic location as a gateway to the Middle East and Africa, Oman offers countless opportunities for businesses to grow and thrive.
Located in Muscat and just 25 minutes from the international airport, AIDA rises 130 metres above sea level as a city within a city, combining nature, hospitality, and a championship golf destination.
