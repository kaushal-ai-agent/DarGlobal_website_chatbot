## Project Overview & Architectural Vision
The luxurious residences redefine living standards with 2-to-4-bedroom marble-accented homes, featuring private outdoor areas, light-filled interiors, and panoramic views of the canal and Downtown Dubai. These meticulously designed spaces combine elegance and functionality, offering an unparalleled living experience.
Signature interiors featuring premium leather, Italian marble, kinetic chandeliers, carbon fibre accents, and lush wood panelling.
Control every aspect of the apartment, from home security, temperature, lighting, music and appliances, all at your fingertips.
From arranging dinner plans to organizing daily housekeeping requests, every need is attended to for residents at all times.
Enjoy a rooftop pool with unparalleled views, plus a gym with separate male and female steam and sauna relaxation space.

## Frequently Asked Questions
**Q: Who developed Da Vinci Tower?**

Da Vinci Tower is developed by DarGlobal in partnership with Pagani Automobili and is the world's first Pagani-branded residential development.

**Q: Where is Da Vinci Tower located?**

Da Vinci Tower is located in Downtown Dubai, directly on the Dubai Canal, with views of the Burj Khalifa and moments from Marasi Business Bay.

**Q: What types of residences are available at Da Vinci Tower?**

The tower offers 2, 3 and 4-bedroom residences ranging from 77 to 398 sq m, each with private outdoor areas and panoramic views of the canal and Downtown Dubai.

**Q: Is Da Vinci Tower really the first Pagani residential project?**

Yes. Da Vinci Tower is the world's first residential development with interiors by Pagani, applying the brand's design philosophy to luxury living.

**Q: What do the Pagani-designed interiors include?**

Interiors crafted by Pagani feature premium leather, Italian marble, kinetic chandeliers, carbon fibre accents and lush wood panelling.

**Q: What amenities does Da Vinci Tower offer?**

Amenities include a rooftop pool overlooking Downtown Dubai, a state-of-the-art gym, sauna and steam rooms, 24/7 concierge, and private access to the residences through discreet elevators.

**Q: How tall is Da Vinci Tower?**

The tower rises 19 floors above the Dubai Canal, with three basement levels and a grand ground floor.

**Q: How far is Da Vinci Tower from key Dubai landmarks?**

The Dubai Mall and Fountains are about 9 minutes away, and Dubai Marina is around 20 minutes away.

**Q: Can international buyers obtain UAE residency by purchasing here?**

Buyers investing AED 2 million or more qualify for the UAE 10-year Golden Visa, and Dubai applies 0% income tax and 0% property tax.

**Q: What makes Da Vinci Tower unique?**

Da Vinci Tower is the world’s first residential development by DarGlobal with interiors designed by Pagani Automobili, bringing the design philosophy of the iconic Italian hypercar manufacturer into luxury living.
