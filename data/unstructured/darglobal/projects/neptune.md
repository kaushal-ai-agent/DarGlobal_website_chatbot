# NEPTUNE, INTERIORS BY MOUAWAD

**Location:** Riyadh, KSA | **Country:** Saudi Arabia  
**Masterplan:** AIDA  
**Brand Partner:** Pagani  
**Status:** Under Development  

## Project Overview & Architectural Vision
Step into a realm where every detail reflects Mouawad's legacy, with refined finishes throughout, each villa is a celebration of luxury and innovation, offering a unique living experience enveloped in the vibrant cultural heritage of Riyadh.
Located in the most sought-after northern part of Riyadh, just moments away from Expo 2030, offering unparalleled access to the city's future landmarks.
Neptune, Interiors by Mouawad is a collection of luxury branded villas in northern Riyadh, developed by DarGlobal. Mouawad, the internationally renowned jewellery house, designed the interiors of each five-bedroom villa, bringing its signature approach to refined finishes, artisanal craftsmanship, and timeless elegance to a residential setting.
Mouawad provided the full interior design across each Neptune villa, applying the brand's heritage in precision and luxury material quality to every room from the entry foyer and living spaces through to the master suite, bathrooms, and kitchen. The collaboration translates Mouawad's jewellery-house sensibility into an architectural and residential context.
Neptune offers five-bedroom villas with built-up areas ranging from 300 to 420 sqm. Each villa includes a master bedroom with walk-in closet and en-suite, secondary bedrooms, a family kitchen, guest living area, and terrace with views of the surrounding landscape.

The Neptune community is supported by schools, a community centre, sports hub, restaurants and cafes, public parks, entertainment centres, early childhood centres, retail and grocery, playgrounds, mosques, and healthcare clinics within the wider neighbourhood.

**Q: Is Riyadh real estate open to foreign investors?**

Yes. Full freehold ownership is now available to foreign investors in Saudi Arabia. Property investment of 4 million SAR or more qualifies buyers for residency eligibility. The Saudi Riyal is pegged to the US Dollar, providing currency stability, and Saudi Arabia applies 0% capital gains tax, 0% wealth tax, and 0% inheritance tax.

**Q: Why invest in Riyadh property under Saudi Vision 2030?**

Riyadh is set to host the FIFA World Cup 2034, Expo 2030, Formula 1, and the Asian Games, driving unprecedented infrastructure investment. King Salman International Airport is scaling to 185 million passengers, and Riyadh Air is expanding global connectivity. Saudi Arabia is considered one of the safest countries globally and the safest in the G20.

**Q: What makes Neptune a limited-edition collection?**

Neptune is positioned as a limited edition collection, meaning a restricted number of villas are available, each featuring bespoke Mouawad interiors and individual design attention that cannot be replicated at scale. The scarcity of a Mouawad-branded residential product in Riyadh places it in a distinct tier above standard luxury villa developments in the city.
