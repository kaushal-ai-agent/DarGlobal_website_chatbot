## Frequently Asked Questions
**Q: What are Trump Golf Villas at AIDA?**

Trump Golf Villas are a collection of five-bedroom luxury villas at AIDA, Muscat, developed by DarGlobal. Each villa is positioned directly along the Trump International Championship Golf Course, 130 metres above the Muscat Riviera, with open views across the fairways and the sea. Every residence includes a branded golf cart for private use across the estate.

**Q: Where are Trump Golf Villas located?**

The villas are within the AIDA estate in Muscat, Oman, 25 minutes from Muscat International Airport. The estate sits in close proximity to a cluster of 5-star hospitality landmarks including the Ritz Carlton, Shangri-La Resort, Nikki Beach, and Jumeirah Muscat Bay Hotel.

**Q: What are the villa sizes and unit types available?**

Trump Golf Villas offer five-bedroom residences from 4,367 sqft. Villas include open terraces and balconies with dual golf and sea views, and each unit comes with a complimentary branded golf cart for use across the AIDA estate. Contact DarGlobal for current unit availability and pricing.

**Q: What amenities does the AIDA estate offer?**

AIDA provides access to the Trump International Golf Club Oman, gated community security, wellness centres, a fitness centre, plazas, cafes and restaurants, hiking trails, mountain biking routes, running trails, a viewing deck, parks and recreational spaces, luxury hotel neighbours, and extensive outdoor landscaping across the cliffside estate.

**Q: When will Trump Golf Villas be completed?**

Completion is scheduled for December 2028. Construction is actively underway. Live progress feeds are available on the DarGlobal project page. Contact the sales team at darglobal.co.uk for current availability and payment plan details.

**Q: Can foreign nationals buy Trump Golf Villas in Oman?**

Yes. Oman offers 100% freehold ownership for all nationalities on qualifying Integrated Tourism Complex (ITC) properties. Buyers in ITC-registered projects are also eligible for a lifetime residency visa. Buyers should confirm specific eligibility conditions with the DarGlobal sales team or their legal advisor.

**Q: What are the tax advantages of buying property in Oman?**

Oman applies 0% personal income tax and 0% inheritance tax, with inheritance assessed under the investor's own country's laws rather than local law. Businesses operating in special economic and free zones benefit from a 25-year exemption from corporate income taxes. The Omani Rial is pegged to the US Dollar, providing currency stability for international investors.

**Q: Why invest in Muscat real estate now?**

Oman is one of the safest countries in the world and ranked 7th globally and 1st in both Asia and the Arab world in the Quality of Life Index by Numbeo in 2023. Oman aims to earn over $22.5 billion a year from tourism by 2040, and an upcoming railway project connecting the UAE to Muscat, alongside the Saudi-Oman Highway, is significantly improving regional connectivity and driving investment.

**Q: How does the Trump Golf Villas product relate to the wider AIDA development?**

AIDA is DarGlobal's flagship cliffside resort development in Muscat, which includes the Trump International Hotel Oman (a hotel room ownership product) as well as the Trump Golf Villas (a standalone residential villa product). The golf villas offer freehold residential ownership of a full five-bedroom villa, with the Trump International Championship Golf Course as an on-doorstep amenity.

Buyers can register interest, book a unit, choose their residence via the online navigator, or download the brochure through DarGlobal at darglobal.co.uk/aida-trump-villas. The sales team can provide current pricing, availability, and payment plan terms on request.
