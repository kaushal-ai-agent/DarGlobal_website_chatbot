## Project Overview & Architectural Vision
In the heart of Muscat, nestled in the hilltops 130m above the shores is AIDA, a magnificent gated community and golfing destination overlooking the world. Its distinctive terrain of curvaceous valleys, sharp cliffs, and endless shores lines perch up a community that blends a vibrant coastal lifestyle with the tranquility of nature.
The Trump Golf Villas are directly situated at the frontlines of the Trump International Golf Club Oman in Aida, Muscat. Owners enjoy a world-class membership to the golf club, as well as, their very own exclusive branded golf cart, empowering residents to enjoy the beautiful game of golf on their own terms.
Introducing The Trump Cliff Villas, an exclusive collection of 3-bedroom residences perched on the cliffside, offering unparalled sea views next to the Trump Hotel
Azure Oceanfront Villas is a limited collection of five-bedroom residences, suspended 130 meters above the sea, inside AIDA, Oman’s most prestigious gated coastal community. Interiors by FENDI Casa. Nothing in front of you. Nothing above you. Only the horizon.
Situated in AIDA, an exclusive gated community and renowned golfing destination, perched 130 meters above the pristine shores, these villas represent an unparalleled investment opportunity. Positioned so close to the sea that you can feel the refreshing ocean breeze, the Coastal Investment Villas offer an exceptional blend of modern design and timeless craftsmanship.

## Frequently Asked Questions
**Q: What is AIDA by DarGlobal and what makes this development unique?**

AIDA is a fully master-planned destination in Muscat, Oman, designed as a city within a city. Developed by DarGlobal, it combines luxury residences, branded hotels, golf courses, nature, and lifestyle amenities in one integrated community.

**Q: Where is AIDA located in Oman?**

AIDA is in Muscat, Oman, about 25 minutes from Muscat International Airport, elevated 130 meters above sea level along the coastline, offering scenic views.

**Q: Can international buyers own property at AIDA?**

Yes. AIDA offers 100% foreign ownership within designated investment zones, ideal for investors and second-home buyers.

**Q: How can I register my interest or book a viewing at AIDA?**

You can register your interest throughour websiteor by visiting one ofour offices.If you are outside Oman, the sales team will contact you and arrange the most suitable way to connect.If you are in Oman or planning a visit, you can visit the office and enjoy a private, one on one site tour, including a visit to the development, the views, and the latest construction updates.

**Q: What types of properties are available at AIDA?**

AIDA offers apartments, luxury villas, hotel residences, and branded or non-branded residences. Explore your options for investment or lifestyle living.

**Q: Are there branded residences at AIDA?**

Yes. AIDA features branded residences in collaboration with internationally recognized brands, including Trump and Marriott.

**Q: Does property ownership include golf access?**

Golf access is available through membership at Trump International Golf Club Oman, subject to application and approval.

**Q: Who is developing AIDA in Oman?**

AIDA is a master plan developed by DarGlobal in partnership with OMRAN, collaborating with global brands such as theTrump OrganizationandMarriott.

**Q: What makes AIDA a city within a city?**

AIDA offers residential living, hospitality, leisure, wellness, retail, and outdoor experiences all in one destination, allowing residents to live, relax, and socialize without leaving the community.

**Q: Is AIDA a gated community in Muscat, Oman?**

Yes. AIDA is a private, gated community in Muscat offering controlled access, security, and a secure residential environment designed for privacy and peace of mind.

**Q: Who is AIDA designed for?**

AIDA is designed for end users, second-home owners, and international investors seeking a luxury resort lifestyle, long-term value, and globally benchmarked living standards in Oman.
