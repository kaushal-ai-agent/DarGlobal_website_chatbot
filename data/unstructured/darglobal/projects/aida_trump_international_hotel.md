## Frequently Asked Questions
**Q: What is Trump International Hotel Oman?**

Trump International Hotel Oman is a luxury hotel at AIDA, Muscat, where investors purchase freehold ownership of individual rooms or suites. The hotel is managed by Trump Hotels, and owners earn a share of revenue from guest bookings while receiving two weeks of complimentary personal use each year.

**Q: What does owning a hotel room at Trump International Hotel Oman mean?**

Owners hold freehold title to a specific room or suite within the hotel. Trump Hotels manages the property professionally, generating rental income from guest bookings. Owners receive a share of that revenue and retain two weeks annually for personal use, giving a tangible asset with managed returns.

**Q: What unit types and sizes are available at Trump International Hotel Oman?**

The hotel offers rooms, junior suites, and suites ranging from 43 to 138 sqm. All units are managed by Trump Hotels as part of the hotel's operational inventory, ensuring professional management and a consistent stream of bookings.

**Q: When will Trump International Hotel Oman be completed?**

Completion is scheduled for December 2028. The hotel is under development within the AIDA master community in Muscat. Contact DarGlobal for current unit availability, pricing, and payment plan details.

**Q: Where is Trump International Hotel Oman located?**

The hotel is within AIDA, a master-planned destination in Muscat, rising 130 metres above sea level and 25 minutes from Muscat International Airport. AIDA includes a championship golf course, beach, night club, wellness centres, hiking and mountain bike trails, retail, and dining venues.

**Q: What are the benefits of hotel room ownership versus residential investment?**

Hotel room ownership generates passive income from guest bookings, comes with full Trump Hotels professional management, and includes two weeks of complimentary personal use per year. Owners hold freehold title to a tangible hospitality asset without the management burden of private rental.

**Q: Is Trump International Hotel Oman developed by the Trump Organization?**

No. The sub-developer is Dar al Arkan Property Development SPC, which uses the Trump name and mark under sublicense from DT Marks Oman LLC. The Trump Organization and its affiliates are not the owners, developers, or sellers of the property.

**Q: What amenities does AIDA offer alongside Trump International Hotel Oman?**

AIDA features the Trump International Golf Club, Trump Member Club, Cliff Night Club, AIDA Beach, hanging suites, sky bar, all-day dining, swimming pools, wellness and fitness centres, hiking and mountain bike trails, a viewing deck, children's splash areas, parks, and promenades.

**Q: Why invest in Oman real estate?**

Oman offers 0% personal income tax, 0% capital gains tax, 0% inheritance tax, 100% freehold ownership for all nationalities within Integrated Tourism Complexes, and lifetime residency visas for qualifying investors. Oman ranked 7th globally in the Numbeo Quality of Life Index 2023, with tourism projected to generate $22.5 billion by 2040.
