## Frequently Asked Questions
**Q: What is Marriott Residences Aida, Oman?**

Marriott Residences Aida, Oman is a fully furnished branded apartment complex at AIDA, Muscat. The project is developed by Dar Al Arkan Property Development SPC, which uses the Marriott name under license from Marriott International. Residences come with à la carte hotel-style services and are designed to deliver the comfort and standard associated with the Marriott brand.

**Q: Who developed Marriott Residences Aida, Oman?**

The project is developed by Dar Al Arkan Property Development SPC, which operates the Marriott brand name under a licensing agreement with Marriott International, Inc. Marriott Residences Aida, Oman are not owned, developed, or sold by Marriott International or its affiliates.

**Q: What unit types and sizes are available?**

The development offers one-, two-, and three-bedroom fully furnished apartments ranging from 60 to 194 sqm. Each residence is designed with contemporary decor and thoughtfully furnished to offer stylish, comfortable living with seamless integration across all spaces.

**Q: What services do residents receive at Marriott Residences?**

Residents benefit from à la carte services styled around the Marriott standard, including a dedicated concierge, 24-hour reception desk, doorman, lobby, and valet. The service model is designed to bring hotel-quality living to private residential ownership at the AIDA estate.

**Q: What amenities does AIDA offer for Marriott Residences owners?**

The AIDA estate provides a championship golf course, swimming pools, a private beach enclave, children's splash areas, wellness centres, fitness centre, plazas, cafes and restaurants, hiking trails, mountain bike trails, running trail, promenades, viewing decks, parks, a clubhouse, outdoor landscapes, and access to nearby 5-star hotels including Nikki Beach, Al Bustan Palace Ritz-Carlton, Shangri-La, and Jumeirah Muscat Bay.

**Q: When will Marriott Residences Aida, Oman be completed?**

Completion is expected in September 2029. The development is currently under construction. Contact DarGlobal at darglobal.co.uk to register interest, book a unit, or download the project brochure.

**Q: Can foreign nationals buy property at Marriott Residences Aida, Oman?**

Yes. Oman offers 100% freehold ownership for all nationalities on qualifying Integrated Tourism Complex (ITC) properties. Buyers on ITC-registered projects are eligible for a lifetime residency visa. An exclusive mortgage program is also available in Oman for both resident and non-resident buyers.

**Q: What are the tax advantages of investing in Oman?**

Oman applies 0% personal income tax, 0% capital gains tax, and 0% inheritance tax, with inheritance assessed under the investor's own country's laws rather than local law. Businesses in special economic and free zones benefit from a 25-year exemption from corporate income taxes.

**Q: Why invest in Muscat real estate now?**

Oman ranked 7th globally and 1st in Asia and the Arab world in the Numbeo Quality of Life Index in 2023, and is one of the safest countries worldwide. The country targets over $22.5 billion per year from tourism by 2040. The Saudi-Oman Highway cuts travel time by 16 hours between the two countries, and an upcoming railway project connecting the UAE to Muscat will further strengthen regional connectivity and investment appeal.

**Q: How does Marriott Residences relate to the wider AIDA development?**

Marriott Residences is one of several residential products within the AIDA estate in Muscat. Other DarGlobal projects at AIDA include Trump International Hotel Oman, Trump Golf Villas, The Great Escape 2, and Sunrise Haven. Together they form a multi-branded, fully integrated resort community 130 metres above the Muscat Riviera.
