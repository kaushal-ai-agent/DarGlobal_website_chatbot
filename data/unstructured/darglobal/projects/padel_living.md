## Project Overview & Architectural Vision
Padel Living Residences is a new residential concept on King Abdulaziz Road in the heart of Jeddah, created for people who thrive on movement, connection, and city life. It brings together contemporary residences, exclusive rooftop padel courts, and a vibrant community within a uniquely connected address.
Padel Living Residences is designed around the energy of an active community, where movement, connection, and well-being are part of everyday life.
Exclusive rooftop padel courts are reserved for residents and available 24/7 through a dedicated booking app, making it easy to stay active and connected.
Located on King Abdulaziz Road, Padel Living Residences places Jeddah's leading dining, retail, and lifestyle destinations within easy reach, bringing together convenience and community.
Designed for a new generation of urban living, Padel Living Residences combines contemporary residences, exclusive amenities, and a vibrant community in one connected address.
