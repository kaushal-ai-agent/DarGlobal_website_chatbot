**Q: What unit types and sizes are available at Marea?**

Marea offers two-, three-, and four-bedroom residences ranging from 223 to 553 sqm. The community is a gated complex within the Finca Cortesin estate, with each unit designed for the panoramic sea and mountain views the site commands.

**Q: When will Marea, Interiors by Missoni be completed?**

Phase 1 of Marea completed in 2024. The full development is scheduled for completion in December 2027. The project is under development by DarGlobal. Contact the sales team for current unit availability, pricing, and payment plan terms.

**Q: Where is Marea, Interiors by Missoni located?**

Marea sits within the Finca Cortesin gated estate in Cortesin, Costa Del Sol. The beach is 5 minutes away, Marbella is 20 minutes, Puerto Banús and Sotogrande are a 20-minute drive, Santa Maria Polo Club is 15 minutes, Gibraltar Airport is 45 minutes, and Malaga Airport is 50 minutes.

**Q: What is the 270-degree view at Marea?**

The site's elevation and orientation give residences an unobstructed 270-degree panorama that stretches from Marbella along the Costa Del Sol coastline, across to Gibraltar, and continues to the mountains of Morocco on the African continent, with the Mediterranean Sea and Finca Cortesin golf course directly below.

**Q: What amenities does Marea, Interiors by Missoni offer?**

Amenities include a gated community with private entrance and 24/7 premium security, 24-hour concierge, infinity pool, state-of-the-art gym, tennis courts, hanging observation deck, community garden, children's playground, children's swimming pool, children's splash area, and dedicated parking and storage per unit.

**Q: What is the Finca Cortesin estate?**

Finca Cortesin is one of Costa Del Sol's most prestigious private estates, home to the five-star Finca Cortesin Hotel and Golf Club. Marea's location within this estate gives residents access to the exclusivity and security of one of Spain's most recognised luxury addresses on the Costa del Sol.

**Q: Why invest in Costa Del Sol property near Cortesin?**

Costa Del Sol combines Spain's highest real estate ROI in Europe with Eurozone interest rates between 3 and 5%, a Mediterranean climate year-round, and strong tourism that supports rental demand. Cortesin's proximity to Marbella, Sotogrande, and Gibraltar positions it within a corridor that draws international buyers and second-home investors consistently.
