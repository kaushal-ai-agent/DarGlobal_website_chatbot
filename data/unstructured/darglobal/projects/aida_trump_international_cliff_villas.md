## Project Overview & Architectural Vision

Under the management of Trump, these furnished villas provide the choice to reside or lease, ensuring the hallmark quality and service associated with the esteemed Trump brand. Living in these villas offers the best sea views and closest access to unparalleled amenities.
Located in Muscat and just 25 minutes from the international airport, AIDA rises 130 metres above sea level as a city within a city, combining nature, hospitality, and a championship golf destination.
Trump Cliff Villas are a collection of fully furnished three-bedroom sea-view villas at AIDA, Muscat, developed by DarGlobal. ## Frequently Asked Questions
**Q: What are Trump Cliff Villas at AIDA?**

Each villa is positioned directly beside the Trump International Hotel at 130 metres above the Muscat Riviera, with a private pool overlooking the sea. Villas are designed and furnished to the highest standard, with every detail specified down to specification.

**Q: What are the villa sizes and unit types available?**

**Q: What does the Trump Cliff Villas offer that other AIDA products do not?**

Trump Cliff Villas offer full villa ownership with a private pool, the closest positioning to the Trump International Hotel, and the most elevated sea views on the AIDA estate. Unlike Trump Golf Villas, which face the golf course, These villas are positioned on the cliff edge with direct sea orientation. Both villa products sit within the same AIDA estate and share access to the same championship golf course and community facilities.

**Q: What amenities are available to Trump Cliff Villa owners?**

Within the AIDA estate, villa owners have access to the Trump International Golf Club Oman (18-hole championship course), Trump Members Only Club, The SHFT Night Club, 5-star dining and culinary facilities, AIDA Beach, Trump Hotels Hanging Suites, a Trump International Hotel lobby, community and retail centres, plazas, cafes and restaurants, wellness centres, fitness centre, promenades, hiking trails, mountain bike trails, running trail, viewing decks, and outdoor landscapes across the cliffside estate.

**Q: When will Trump Cliff Villas be completed?**

Completion is scheduled for December 2028. Construction is underway and live progress feeds are available on the DarGlobal project page. Contact DarGlobal at darglobal.co.uk to book a unit, choose your residence via the online navigator, or download the project brochure.

**Q: Can I lease my Trump Cliff Villa when I am not using it?**

Yes. The entry data states that owners have the choice to reside in or lease their villa. The Trump management association with the property supports the leasing proposition. Contact DarGlobal for further details on the leasing model and revenue arrangements.

**Q: Can foreign nationals buy Trump Cliff Villas in Oman?**

Yes. Oman offers 100% freehold ownership for all nationalities on qualifying Integrated Tourism Complex (ITC) properties. ITC project buyers are eligible for a lifetime residency visa in Oman. An exclusive mortgage program is available in Oman for both resident and non-resident buyers. Buyers should confirm specific eligibility conditions with DarGlobal or their legal advisor.

**Q: What are the tax advantages of investing in Oman real estate?**

Oman applies 0% personal income tax, 0% capital gains tax, and 0% inheritance tax, with inheritance assessed under the investor's own country's laws. Businesses in special economic and free zones benefit from a 25-year exemption from corporate income taxes.

**Q: Why invest in Muscat property now?**

Oman ranked 7th globally and 1st in both Asia and the Arab world in the Numbeo Quality of Life Index in 2023, and is one of the safest countries in the world. Tourism earnings are projected to exceed $22.5 billion per year by 2040. The Saudi-Oman Highway cuts travel time between the two countries by 16 hours, and an upcoming UAE-Muscat railway project will further strengthen connectivity and investment activity.

**Q: How do Trump Cliff Villas relate to the wider AIDA development?**

Trump Cliff Villas are one of several residential products within the AIDA estate in Muscat. Other DarGlobal products at AIDA include Trump International Hotel Oman (hotel room ownership), Trump Golf Villas (five-bedroom fairway villas), Marriott Residences Aida, The Great Escape 2, and Sunrise Haven. Together they form a fully integrated branded resort community 130 metres above the Muscat Riviera.
