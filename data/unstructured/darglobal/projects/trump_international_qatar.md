**Location:** SIMAISMA, QATAR | **Country:** Qatar

## Project Overview & Architectural Vision
DarGlobal and The Trump Organization, in collaboration with Qatari Diar, bring Trump International Simaisma, Qatar to Qatar’s eastern coast. Set within the 8 million sqm Simaisma destination, the project is surrounded by 7 km of waterfront, with access to leisure, hospitality, entertainment, golf, marina, dining and retail experiences.
Classical, Andalusian, and Contemporary Villas
Part of an evolving waterfront destination, Trump International Simaisma, Qatar combines privacy with convenient access to golf, leisure, family attractions, healthcare and the surrounding natural landscapes.
Featuring a dedicated Community Centre exclusively for residents, and Beach Club.
