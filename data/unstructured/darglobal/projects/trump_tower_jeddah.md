# TRUMP TOWER JEDDAH

**Location:** Jeddah Corniche, Saudi Arabia | **Country:** Saudi Arabia  
**Masterplan:** AIDA  
**Brand Partner:** Pagani  
**Status:** Under Development  

## Project Overview & Architectural Vision
The interiors of Trump Tower Jeddah seamlessly combine modern elegance with timeless sophistication, creating spaces that exude refinement and comfort. Designed to complement its breathtaking Red Sea views, each detail reflects a commitment to luxurious living.
Located along Jeddah’s picturesque Red Sea coastline, Trump Tower Jeddah offers breathtaking sea views and proximity to the city’s top landmarks. With easy access to the marina, Formula 1 racetrack, and iconic fountain, it’s a location that seamlessly combines coastal beauty with urban convenience.
Trump Tower Jeddah is a 47-floor Trump-branded seafront residential tower on the Jeddah Corniche, developed by DarGlobal. It offers one- to four-bedroom apartments and three- and four-bedroom penthouses with direct Red Sea views, alongside the Trump Private Club for residents and members.
The tower offers one-, two-, three-, and four-bedroom apartments as well as three- and four-bedroom penthouses, ranging from 70 to 365 sqm. All units are positioned on the Jeddah Corniche and face the Red Sea.
Completion is scheduled for December 2029. The project is under development by DarGlobal. Buyers can contact the sales team directly for current availability, payment plan structure, and brochure download.

**Q: Why invest in Jeddah real estate under Saudi Vision 2030?**

Saudi Arabia offers 0% income tax and capital gains tax, a 30-year corporate tax exemption, and premium Saudi residency eligibility for investments of 4 million SAR or more. The SAR is pegged to the US dollar, and Saudi Arabia hosts Expo 2030 and the 2034 FIFA World Cup.

**Q: What makes Trump Tower Jeddah's location on the Corniche valuable?**

The Jeddah Corniche is the city's most sought-after coastal address. The tower's seafront position, proximity to the marina and F1 circuit, and its Standing as one of the tallest buildings on the strip place it at the centre of Jeddah's long-term urban and tourism growth corridor.
