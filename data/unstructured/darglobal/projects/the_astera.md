## Project Overview & Architectural Vision
Each seafront residence is a true work of art. From every angle, you'll witness the obsessive attention to detail that embodies Aston Martin's unwavering dedication to precision and beauty. A true testament to living excellence.
The Astera, Interiors by Aston Martin is located on Al Marjan Island, a shining gem on the coastline of Ras Al Khaimah, UAE. This remarkable man-made island offers a luxurious retreat surrounded by pristine beaches and crystal-clear waters. Boasting an array of world-class resorts, upscale residences, and vibrant leisure attractions.
The Astera, Interiors by Aston Martin is a beachfront residential development on Al Marjan Island, Ras Al Khaimah, developed by DarGlobal. Aston Martin's design team applied the brand's precision and craftsmanship to each unit, producing a collection of one- to three-bedroom residences and three-bedroom beach villas directly on the water's edge.
The Astera offers one-, two-, and three-bedroom residences and three-bedroom beachfront villas ranging from 61 to 265 sqm. All units feature Aston Martin-designed interiors and sit on Al Marjan Island with direct beach access.
Aston Martin's design team was responsible for the interiors of every residence, applying the brand's approach to precision, material quality, and artisanal craftsmanship to kitchens, bathrooms, and living spaces. The collaboration integrates cutting-edge design with the timeless finishes associated with the Aston Martin brand.
